##Persona 1

## Winston, the staff member

> "Every customer deserves to feel valued and leave my shop satisfied, even on the busiest days."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo or sketch** | Winston-Photo.jpeg |
| **Age, role and background** | A 42-year-old expert cellphone technician with a repair shop in Iligan City. Single-handedly handles customer inquiries, phone repairs, and daily shop operations. |
| **Goals** | 1. Organize customers according to their arrival. 2. Reduce overcrowding and confusion inside the shop.3. Keep track of pending repairs and customers who are waiting for service.|
| **Frustrations** | 1. Customers repeatedly ask how long they need to wait. 2. Customers become frustrated when I don't immediately entertain them. 3. Customers leaving without having their device repaired because of spending much time repairing a different phone|
| **Devices and context** | Uses a desktop computer at the counter to accomodate customer messages while also repairing phones, occasionally uses a smartphone . | 
| **Tech comfort** | HIGH|
| **Accessibility needs** | blurry left eye, hands and eyes always busy, sometimes shop becomes crowded and noisy |

### Scenario

On a busy afternoon in his shop, Winston is soldering a tiny circuit board while reviewing a list of customers already waiting in the online queue. Sensing he is just minutes away from finishing the delicate repair, Winston glances at his counter desktop and makes a one-click action through the app to notify the next person in line. As he places the final component and reassembles the phone, the notified customer walks into the shop right on cue, allowing Winston to hand off the completed repair and seamlessly serve the next client without any waiting room congestion.


### Top tasks in the app

| Task | Screen | Requirement ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check and notify customers in queue | Staff Dashboard | FR-01 |
| Check how many people are ahead | My place| FR-02 |
| Join/Withdraw from queue | My place | FR-03 |

### What this means for my interface

1. Because winston is very busy, my interface must have a large and very noticeable button for notifying the next customer that it takes a second for winston to see it
2. Because winston has blurry left eye and busy, my interface must only have a few buttons to minimize the risk of Winston accidentally pressing the wrong button while working.
3. Because he is already old and have eye condition, I generally need to make the fonts larger than usual.

##Persona 2

## Rey, the person waiting

> "I don’t want to travel all the way to Cagayan de Oro only to find out that the shop is too busy."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo or sketch** | Rey-Photo.jpeg |
| **Age, role and background** | A 3rd Year BS IT student from Iligan City. |
| **Goals** | 1. Find a cellphone repair shop with reliable and trusted technician.  2. A way to know whether he can be accommodated before traveling to another city.|
| **Frustrations** | 1. The big well known and trusted repair shop in Cagayan de Oro is known to have many customers waiting. 2. He risks wasting time and transportation expenses without receiving service. |
| **Devices and context** | Uses a laptop, relies on Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot to connect to the internet | 
| **Tech comfort** | HIGH|
| **Accessibility needs** | Broken phone, needs Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot to connect to the internet using his Laptop|

### Scenario

Rey needs his cellphone repaired, but he doesn't trust local repair shops in Iligan City. He opens the queuing app on his laptop to check whether the trusted repair shop in Cagayan de Oro has available slots, he checks the number of customers in the queue, and the estimated waiting time before joining the queue. After securing his place, Rey monitors his queue status through the app while waiting at home. When his turn is approaching, he travels to Cagayan de Oro at the appropriate time, minimizing unnecessary waiting at the shop. Upon arrival, the technician inspects his cellphone and begins the repair, allowing Mark to save valuable time and transportation expenses.


### Top tasks in the app

| Task | Screen | Requirement ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Check how many people are in the current queue | My Place | FR-01 |
| Join the queue and receive a ticked ID | My place | FR-02 |
| Show waiting list in order | Staff Dashboard | FR-03 |

### What this means for my interface

 **What this means for my interface** 
1. Because Rey has no mobile data while his phone is broken, the interface must work well on laptops and load quickly over Wi-Fi or a mobile hotspot.
2. Because Rey is checking his queue position in Iligan before traveling to Cagayan de Oro, his queue position must the first thing he see and the biggest when opening the app. 
3. Because Rey might be in class while checking for his queue position with a laptop, the interface must allow him to see his queue position very quickly with almost one tap to avoid instructor noticing him.
